Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device


The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.

The Application collects your device's location, which helps the Service Provider determine your approximate geographical location and make use of in below ways:

  • Geolocation Services: The Service Provider utilizes location data to provide features such as personalized content, relevant recommendations, and location-based services.
  • Analytics and Improvements: Aggregated and anonymized location data helps the Service Provider to analyze user behavior, identify trends, and improve the overall performance and functionality of the Application.
  • Third-Party Services: Periodically, the Service Provider may transmit anonymized location data to external services. These services assist them in enhancing the Application and optimizing their offerings.

The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices and marketing promotions.


When you sign in, the Application processes your account identifier, email address, display name, and optional profile photo to provide your account and calendar sharing. Your records, labels, notes, sharing relationships, and notification settings are stored in Firebase when you use the corresponding cloud features. Guest records remain on your device until you choose to sign in and sync them.


Sharing Records with Other Users

The Application includes optional features that let other people see what you recorded. These features are off unless you turn them on, and each one is controlled by you.

  • Shared calendars: if you accept an invitation from another user, that user can see the records on the shared calendar. You can stop sharing at any time.
  • Community records: after accepting the community notice, you may turn on "Show my records to everyone" in Settings. Records saved while this setting is on are shown to other users. Notes are included only when the separate note-sharing setting is enabled. Other users see only the stamp you chose, its label, the note you attached if you added one, and the time at which it was posted. They do not see your name, your email address, your account identifier, the calendar date of the record, or your private records. The server uses a random internal author identifier to implement reporting and blocking; it is not a public account profile. The contents of a note or custom label may nevertheless identify you, so do not include personal information.
  • Cheers: other users can send a single cheer on a record. For community records the sender is not identified to you.


In version 2.2.0, a community record is stored as a separate copy and remains visible until you make it private, delete the original, or it is removed through moderation. Turning "Show my records to everyone" off withdraws all your currently public records. Editing the original updates the public copy. Users who accepted the earlier 48-hour notice must accept the updated notice before creating records without a time limit. Copies created under that earlier notice keep their original expiration.


Reporting and Blocking

If you report a community record, the Service Provider stores your account identifier, the reported record, and the reason you selected, so that reports can be reviewed and repeated abuse can be identified. If you choose not to see a particular user, the Service Provider stores the pair of account identifiers involved so that neither of you is shown the other. Reports and blocks are retained for as long as is needed to operate and moderate the service. The other user is not told that you reported or blocked them.


Notifications

If you enable reminders or other notifications, the Application stores a push notification token for your device together with the language and time zone you use, so that notifications can be delivered at the right time and in the right language. You can turn notifications off at any time in the Application or in your device settings.


Third Party Access

Firebase (Google) processes account information, cloud records, profile images, device notification tokens, and technical service information to operate sign-in, storage, sharing, and notifications. Sentry processes error reports and technical diagnostic information to identify failures. Google AdMob may process IP-derived approximate location, device or app-scoped identifiers, ad interactions, crash information, and performance information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Version 2.2.0 requests non-personalized ads, disables the publisher first-party advertising identifier, and does not request access to IDFA. Where consent is required, ads are requested only after the consent flow permits them. A privacy-options control is provided when required so you can revise your choice. The Application does not access GPS location.
